Getting around

Arrival: airport to Golem

Easiest for the group: two taxis or a pre-booked minivan straight to Golem. Published fares are about €40–48 per car, 45–60 minutes. The route goes west via Vorë and Durrës and does not pass through central Tirana, so the protests don't affect it.

  • Agree the price before loading bags. Ask "Sa kushton deri në Golem?" and confirm lek or euro.
  • Drivers who approach you inside the terminal tend to quote high, often in euros.
  • Uber doesn't operate in Albania.

Budget option: airport bus to Durrës bus station, 600 lek cash. Listed departures from the airport: 10:00 · 12:00 · 14:00 · 15:30 · 17:00 · 19:00 · 21:00 (check at the airport bus bays). From Durrës, a taxi to Golem is 1,500–2,000 lek, or take the Golem bus from the roadside by the station (100 lek).

Golem ⇄ Tirana

Intercity buses heading north stop at the Golem roundabout. Timetables list departures from 06:48, €3–8, about 40–50 min, arriving at Tirana's East Terminal or the North & South Terminal. Both are outside the centre, so add a taxi or city bus.

Back: from the North & South Terminal, take a bus towards Kavajë and ask the driver to stop in Golem (listed departures roughly 07:00–17:00, 300 lek). Aim to leave Tirana before dark, which is also when protests pick up.

Airport ⇄ Tirana centre: hourly bus, 400 lek, stops behind the Opera on Skanderbeg Square, inside the protest zone.

Taxis

  • Durrës → Golem: 1,500–2,000 lek (€15–20).
  • Airport or Tirana → Golem: about €40–48.
  • Always agree the fare first and pay in lek. Show the taxi card on the Home tab.

Money

Converter

What works best

  • Bring euro notes and change them at a city exchange office. Many offices charge no commission. Ask "Pa komision?" and the rate before handing over cash, and count it at the counter.
  • Skip the airport counters and ATMs. r/tirana users report clearly worse rates there.
  • ATMs all charge foreign cards, usually 600–900 lek per withdrawal. Take out larger amounts less often. One Reddit user found Union Bank cheapest (about 500 lek).
  • Always decline the conversion ("Decline / Without conversion" or "Charge in ALL") at ATMs and card terminals. Accepting it adds the bank's own markup.
  • Euros are often accepted, but at a poor rate. Pay buses, cafés and small shops in lek; supermarkets and hotels take cards.
  • Old lek: people sometimes quote prices ×10 (a 1,000-lek meal said as "10,000"). Ask "Në lekë të rinj?"

Where to change

Golem: the Western Union exchange and Lika, both on Rruga e Detit, on the way to the beach. Opening hours aren't published; small offices usually work daytime and keep shorter hours off-season, so ask when you pass and write it in your notes. ATMs are along Rruga e Detit and the promenade.

Durrës: several offices in the centre around Rruga Fetah Bllaca and Rruga Kristaq Rama.

Tirana: Iliria'98 is the chain Reddit recommends most. Its Rruga e Kavajës and Blloku branches are near the protest zone, so go in daytime.

Safety & avoid

Protests in Tirana (ongoing)
Since
13 Oct 2026; 22nd day on 3 Nov. Students and young people demanding PM Rama's resignation.
Where
Skanderbeg Square, Dëshmorët e Kombit Boulevard, the PM's office and Parliament, the Ministry of Education (Rruga e Durrësit). Red zones on the map.
When
Crowds build late afternoon and evening; clashes have mostly been after dark.
What
Tear gas, water cannon and rubber bullets; petrol bombs and fireworks thrown. 272 injured in the first week. Shops in the centre pull down shutters.
Elsewhere
Smaller protests reported in Shkodër, Elbasan and Korçë. Nothing reported in Golem.
Advice
The UK says avoid demonstrations and large gatherings in central Tirana; it does not advise against travel. If you see a crowd or police lines, walk away and check the group chat.

Check before going to Tirana

The red zones on the map are the usual hotspots as of 3 Nov and don't update themselves. No source publishes protest locations as live data, but these post each one before it starts, with the time and place:

  • U.S. Embassy Tirana: "Demonstration Alert" notices naming streets and times, usually a day ahead.
  • Euronews Albania (English) and Albanian Daily News: same-day reports on where crowds are heading.
  • GOV.UK Albania advice: the overall picture.

If you spot trouble, long-press the map and tick "Area to avoid" to warn the others for 12 hours.

Avoid list

  • Airport exchange counters and ATMs — worse rates than the city.
  • Taxi drivers who approach you in the terminal — fares quoted high in euros.
  • Bars where staff sit down with you or order for you — the bill can be several times normal. Ask for a priced menu and pay per round.
  • Fish priced per 100 g or per kilo — ask "per portion or per kilo?" and see the fish before it's cooked. Decline bread or salad you didn't order, or ask the price.
  • Old-lek prices — if a number sounds ten times too high, ask "new lek?"
  • Lower-rated Golem restaurants on TripAdvisor: Sea Zone (3.3), Arome Deti (3.7), Ujvara (3.9).
  • Unofficial "guides" around Skanderbeg Square who steer you to shops for a commission.

Night out

  • Agree a return plan before the last bus; a taxi home from Durrës is 1,500–2,000 lek.
  • Post "Home safe" in the group chat when you get back.
  • Keep the taxi card one tap away: Home tab → Show to driver.

Offline setup

Do this on Wi-Fi before going out

  • Organic Maps (free, Android and iOS) works fully offline with walking directions. Download the Albania map in the app, then open the pins file golem-pins.kml shared in the group to load home and every place in this app.
  • Google Maps: download an offline area covering Golem and Durrës, and save the home address as a starred place.
  • Screenshot the taxi card (Home → Show to driver). It works with no battery-hungry apps.
  • This page: once loaded it keeps working in an open tab with no signal. Don't close or refresh the tab while offline.
  • Fully offline copy: golem-basecamp.html, shared in the group. On Android, save it and open it in Chrome; it then works with no internet at all, including the GPS arrow. On iPhone, keep this online page open in a Safari tab instead.

The live group chat needs internet; everything else in this app is built in. Without signal, messages wait in the outbox, and you can pass the ledger between phones (Chat tab).

Chat with no internet at all

A web page can't link phones directly. If you'll be out of signal together a lot, install one of these native apps. They pass messages phone to phone over Bluetooth, no internet or SIM needed:

  • bitchat: Bluetooth mesh chat, iPhone and Android. Range is roughly 10 to 100 m per hop, and phones relay for each other.
  • Briar: encrypted messenger that syncs over Bluetooth or shared Wi-Fi. Android only.
